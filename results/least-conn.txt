Even distribution (~1000 each), effectively identical to round-robin.

Why: least_conn routes each request to the backend with the fewest
active connections. All three backends here are identical and respond
equally fast, so none ever accumulates a connection backlog — the
"fewest connections" is always a near-tie and it behaves like
round-robin. The two methods diverge only when backends respond at
different speeds: a slow backend holds connections open longer, so
least_conn steers new requests away from it while round-robin keeps
feeding it blindly.
